How it works
  • Neurons are tokens. A TICK token is one 0.1 ms step. spk_j means neuron j fired. Commands and senses are registers. Qwen's answer or a DRIVE token from the world writes them.
  • The CNS is three stock Qwen3.5 Gated DeltaNet layers on Qwen's own 1,572,864-wide residual. Layer 1 holds the registers. Layer 2 is a 23-slot spike ring. Its 19-slot read passes through the connectome and gives the 1.8 ms delay. Layer 3 holds every membrane. Logits are membrane potentials. Greedy decoding is the spike threshold.
  • One stack. Qwen's 32 layers and the fly's 3 share one residual, one vocabulary and one token stream. On fly tokens the weights mute Qwen exactly. On text tokens they keep the fly from writing. Checked against a float64 executor that matches Brian2 spike for spike.
  • Qwen's answer drives the fly. At the report's last token the fly's first layer reads Qwen's final hidden state. Forward, left, right, back, jump, fly and groom each read their logit over stop (jump and fly over forward). Each drives one descending neuron type: P9, DNa02, MDN, the giant fiber, DNg02 or aDN. Left and right inhibit each other so only the favoured side turns, by its margin. A strong pull to either side stops the walk, so the fly turns in place.
  • The fly prints its senses. After each scaffold word a SAY token lets the fly's layers pick the next word. LM-head rows read its taste neurons, MN9, Johnston's organ and flight motor neurons. Qwen then reads those words.
  • Light. Glow is membrane potential from the logits. A spike flashes and sends out a ring. Halos mark the groups the bridge panel follows. The wall shows each layer's 48 strongest of 9,216 neurons, 8 to a column.
  • Body and world sit outside the neurons as in NeuroMechFly. The body reads nerve-cord pools, the jump and flight motor neurons and MN9. It never reads the driven neurons. Gait and wingbeat are drawn.
  • Body: flybody (Vaxenburg et al. 2025). Connectome: Janelia male CNS v1.0 in Shiu et al.'s 2024 model, Kenyon-cell loops removed, counts scaled by 0.73.
